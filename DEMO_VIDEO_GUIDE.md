# 🎬 2-Minute Demo Video Recording Guide & Pitch Script
## Project: Infrastructure Failure Chain Predictor (IFCP)

---

## ⚡ Option A: Automated 1-Click Screen Recording (Zero Effort!)

Humne web application ke andar hi ek **"🎬 Auto-Play Demo Walkthrough"** engine daal diya hai jo:
* Map ko automatically pan karta hai.
* Rainfall slider ko cloudburst (130mm) par set karta hai.
* Cascade simulation run karta hai ($T=0$ to $T=60\text{m}$).
* Computer ke AI speaker se **English narration khud bolta hai** (Web Speech API).
* Screen ke neeche sleek **Presentation Subtitles & Progress Bar** dikhata hai.
* Asian Heart Hospital ko select karke XAI causal chain dikhata hai.
* Interventions (Pumps & Green Corridor) activate karke hospital rescue dikhata hai!

### 🎥 Record Karne Ke 3 Simple Steps:
1. Terminal mein `python run.py` chalao (Browser open hoga: `http://127.0.0.1:5000`).
2. Screen recorder start karo:
   * **Windows Shortcut:** Press **`Windows Key + Alt + R`** (Windows Game Bar instant screen recording shuru kar deta hai).
   * Ya Chrome mein **Loom Extension** ya **OBS Studio** use karo.
3. Top navigation bar mein purple button dabao: **"🎬 Auto-Play Demo Walkthrough"**.
4. 80 seconds tak screen ko dekho — app khud ko visually demonstrate karega aur bolkar explain karega!
5. Jab complete ho jaye, recording stop karo (**`Win + Alt + R`** again). Tumhara **ready-to-submit 1080p demo video** tayyar hai!

## 🎙️ Option B: Agar Tum Apni Awaaz Mein Record Karna Chahte Ho (Script)

Agar tum khud bolkar video record karna chahte ho, toh neeche **exact 2-minute word-by-word pitch script** hai:

---

### ⏱️ Timestamp: 0:00 - 0:25 (The Problem Hook)
> **[Action: Map par Mumbai BKC basin dikhao, saare nodes green hain]**
> 
> *"Namaste Sir and friends! Modern smart cities mein jab extreme weather aata hai, toh sabse badi problem hoti hai **Cascading Failures** — yaani ek system ka doobna doosre connected systems ko tabah kar deta hai. Heavy rain se drainage fail hota hai, drainage se roads paani mein doobti hain, power substations trip hote hain, aur sabse dangerous — **trauma hospitals tak ambulances ka rasta band ho jata hai!** Departments alag-alag kaam karte hain, isliye koi is chain reaction ko pehle se predict nahi kar pata. Humne banaya hai **IFCP: Infrastructure Failure Chain Predictor**."*

---

### ⏱️ Timestamp: 0:25 - 0:50 (The Trigger & Cascade Simulation)
> **[Action: Slider ko 130mm/h par le jao aur 'Play Cascade' button dabao]**
> 
> *"Ab dekhiye ek live disaster simulation. Hum Mumbai coastal basin par 130 mm/h ka cloudburst trigger karte hain aur 'Play Cascade' start karte hain. Timeline advance hoti hai: T+10m par Mithi river ka floodgate overflow hota hai. T+20m par Kalanagar highway bottleneck 45cm paani mein doob jata hai. T+35m par Kalina power substation flood ho kar trip ho jata hai. Aur T+45m par sabse bada hazard — Asian Heart Institute trauma hospital ka emergency corridor red ho kar sever ho jata hai!"*

---

### ⏱️ Timestamp: 0:50 - 1:15 (Explainable AI - XAI Diagnosis)
> **[Action: Map par 'Asian Heart Institute' par click karo, right sidebar mein XAI panel open hoga]**
> 
> *"Humara AI sirf risk nahi batata, balki **Explainable AI (XAI)** ke zariye Root Cause diagnose karta hai. Right panel mein dekhiye: System ne reverse causal graph trace karke bataya ki Asian Heart Hospital kyun isolate hua — Mithi Outfall breach $\to$ Kalanagar submerged $\to$ Ambulance route blocked. Saath hi SHAP attribution batata hai ki 52% vulnerability rainfall ki wajah se hai aur 24% siltation ki wajah se."*

---

### ⏱️ Timestamp: 1:15 - 1:40 (The 'What-If' Preventive Countermeasures)

> 
> *"Ab humara sabse bada differentiator: **Actionable Countermeasures!** Incident commander baadh aate hi 'Mobile Dewatering Pumps' aur 'Police Ambulance Green Corridor' activate karta hai. Model instant re-simulate karta hai aur dekhiye — System Resilience Index turant +15.2% recover ho gaya aur Asian Heart Hospital corridor wapas GREEN (Safe) ho gaya!"*
